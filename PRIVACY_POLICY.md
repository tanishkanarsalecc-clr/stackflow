# StackFlow Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 21, 2026

StackFlow ("StackFlow", "we", "us", or "our") is a business management application designed to help businesses manage inventory, products, customers, suppliers, sales, invoices, and business-related reports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected or stored when you use the StackFlow application, why that information is used, how it is protected, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By using StackFlow, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The information collected by StackFlow depends on the features you use and the information you choose to provide.

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create or use a StackFlow account, we may process:

* Email address
* Firebase Authentication account identifier
* Authentication and account-related information
* Login and session information

Your account password is handled by Firebase Authentication. StackFlow does not intentionally store your password in its application database.

### 1.2 Business and Profile Information

If you use the business profile or settings features, you may provide:

* Business name
* Business phone number
* Business email address
* Business address
* GST number or other tax information
* Profile picture or avatar
* Other business information that you voluntarily enter

This information is used to provide business management and billing functionality.

### 1.3 Product and Inventory Information

When you add and manage products, StackFlow may store:

* Product name
* Product category
* Buying/cost price
* Selling price
* Stock quantity
* Low-stock alert value
* Product images
* Other product information entered by you

This information is used to manage inventory, stock levels, product searches, and billing.

### 1.4 Customer Information

When you create customer records, StackFlow may process information that you provide, including:

* Customer name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Business or contact information
* Information associated with customer invoices

You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate legal basis or permission to enter and use personal information belonging to your customers.

### 1.5 Supplier Information

When you use supplier management features, StackFlow may process:

* Supplier name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Business information
* Other supplier information entered by you

This information is used to manage supplier records.

### 1.6 Billing and Invoice Information

When you create bills, sales records, or invoices, StackFlow may process:

* Invoice number
* Invoice date
* Products and quantities
* Product prices
* Subtotal
* Discounts
* Taxes
* Total amount
* Payment status
* Customer information associated with an invoice
* Other invoice information entered by you

This information is used to create invoices, maintain sales records, calculate totals, and generate business reports.

### 1.7 Images and Media

StackFlow may allow you to upload or select images for features such as:

* Profile pictures
* Product images
* Other images supported by the application

Depending on the feature you use and the permissions granted on your Android device, StackFlow may request access to your camera or photos/media.

Camera or photo/media access is used only when required for a feature that you choose to use.

## 2. How We Use Information

StackFlow may use the information described above to:

* Create and manage user accounts
* Authenticate users securely
* Maintain login sessions
* Manage products and inventory
* Track stock quantities and low-stock information
* Manage customer records
* Manage supplier records
* Create and manage bills and invoices
* Calculate prices, taxes, discounts, and totals
* Track payment status
* Generate sales reports and business summaries
* Store and display product and profile images
* Provide PDF invoice generation and related functionality
* Provide printing-related functionality where supported
* Provide application support
* Investigate and resolve technical issues
* Maintain the security and reliability of the application
* Improve application functionality and performance

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Firebase and Cloud Services

StackFlow uses Firebase services provided by Google to support important application functionality.

Depending on the version and configuration of the application, these services include:

### Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is used to create and authenticate user accounts, including email/password login.

### Cloud Firestore

Cloud Firestore is used to store application records such as:

* Products
* Inventory information
* Customers
* Suppliers
* Invoices
* Sales information
* Business profile information
* Other information required for StackFlow functionality

### Firebase Storage

Firebase Storage is used to store uploaded files and images, such as product images and profile images.

Firebase services are operated by Google. Information processed through these services may be subject to Google's privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Device Permissions

StackFlow may request certain Android permissions when you choose to use features that require them.

Depending on the application version and enabled features, these may include:

### Camera

Camera access may be requested when you choose to capture an image, such as a profile or product image.

### Photos and Media

Photo/media access may be requested when you choose an existing image from your device.

You can manage or revoke applicable permissions through your Android device settings.

If you deny an optional permission, some features that depend on that permission may not function, but the core application may remain available.

## 5. Notifications

If StackFlow provides notification functionality and you enable it, the application may use notifications to provide relevant application information or reminders.

You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

## 6. Reports, Analytics, and Application Information

StackFlow processes your business records to provide reports and summaries, including information such as:

* Total sales
* Invoice counts
* Paid and unpaid invoices
* Items sold
* Discounts
* Taxes
* Sales totals
* Other calculations based on information stored in your account

If additional analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostic services are introduced in a future version of StackFlow, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

## 7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

StackFlow does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed only when reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

* Providing StackFlow's features and services
* Operating Firebase and other infrastructure used by the application
* Providing technical or customer support
* Protecting the security of the application
* Preventing fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access
* Complying with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful government requests
* Protecting the rights, property, or safety of StackFlow, its users, or others

Where third-party service providers are used to support StackFlow, they may process information only as reasonably necessary to provide their services and subject to appropriate contractual or confidentiality obligations where applicable.

These measures may include:

* Firebase Authentication for account authentication
* Access controls for cloud-stored data
* Secure cloud infrastructure provided by Firebase/Google
* Appropriate protection of uploaded files and images
* Reasonable measures designed to prevent unauthorized access

However, no electronic storage system, internet transmission, or application can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and for maintaining the security of your device.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

* Provide StackFlow's services
* Maintain your account and business records

* Meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your StackFlow account and associated personal information.

To request account or data deletion, contact us using the support email provided in the **Contact Us** section below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a deletion request.

When deletion is completed, information associated with your account may be permanently deleted from active systems, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal, tax, accounting, security, or regulatory requirements.

Some information may therefore be retained when retention is legally required or reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes.
